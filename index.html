NATHANAEL HEATH

I am a current international student at Amherst College, majoring in Economics and Sociology. As a senior, my academic life has largely culminated in the social sciences, though I have been quite creative since I was a child. I would draw characters I watched on TV, and have always had a keen eye for design and aesthetics. It would be until my junior year in college, however, that I would take a formal studio course. Since then, I have endeavored to incorporate art practice into my schedule each semester.

Artist research, found-object reflection, and an analysis of the function of your box.

Color studies
