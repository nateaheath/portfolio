Living Artist

Milton Courts

Milton Courts (b.2004) is a multidisciplinary artist from Baltimore, who currently resides in Limón, Costa Rica. He started his painting practice in high school, soon after moving to Costa Rica where he now explores painting, stone sculpture, and carved wood. Similar to my art practice, Courts explores “memory, identity, and the stories embedded in people and materials.”
